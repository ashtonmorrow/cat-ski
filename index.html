Cat Ski is a visual downhill skiing game. A browser with Canvas support is needed to play.

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, depending on your chosen controls. Up slows; down speeds up. Space starts or retries, P pauses or resumes, and 1, 2, 3 perform air tricks. On touch screens, use the joystick and trick buttons.
